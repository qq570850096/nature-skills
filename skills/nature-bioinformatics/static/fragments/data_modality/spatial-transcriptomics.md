# Data modality: spatial transcriptomics

## Required context

- Platform, tissue section design, spot/cell resolution, number of sections and
  biological samples.
- Spatial preprocessing, image registration, segmentation or deconvolution, and
  coordinate handling.
- Region, niche, or anatomical annotation rules.
- Link between spatial signals and histology, marker genes, immunostaining, or
  orthogonal validation when available.

## Publication logic

Spatial data can support location, neighborhood, co-localization, and regional
expression claims. It does not prove direct physical interaction or causal
communication without additional validation.

## Common reviewer risks

- Claiming "interaction" from proximity alone.
- Omitting section/sample replication.
- Showing attractive tissue maps without quantification.
- Failing to separate image-derived annotation from transcriptomic inference.
- Ignoring spatial autocorrelation or region-size imbalance.
