# Data modality: single-cell multiomics

## Required context

- Which modalities are paired or unpaired: RNA, ATAC, protein, TCR/BCR,
  perturbation, methylation, or spatial layer.
- Cell matching, modality alignment, peak/gene linking, antibody panel, or
  integration method.
- Missing-modality handling and whether the same cells, samples, or matched
  cohorts support the claim.

## Publication logic

Multiomics can strengthen regulatory or cell-state claims when modalities
converge. It can also multiply failure modes when integration is underreported.

## Common reviewer risks

- Treating unpaired modalities as direct single-cell evidence.
- Claiming regulatory mechanism from correlation without perturbation or motif
  support.
- Omitting modality-specific QC and batch variables.
- Using a black-box integration result without showing modality agreement.
