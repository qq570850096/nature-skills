# Analysis claim: spatial niche

## Safe claim structure

`Spatial analysis identified [region/neighborhood] enriched for [cell states or
genes], associated with [phenotype], with [replication/validation boundary].`

## Required support

- Definition of niche, region, neighborhood, or distance threshold.
- Quantification across samples or sections.
- Link to histology, pathology annotation, image features, or marker validation.
- Control for region size, tissue composition, or spatial autocorrelation when
  relevant.

## Risk controls

- Proximity does not prove physical or functional interaction.
- Attractive maps need quantification and sample-level support.
- Avoid clinical implication without cohort-level association or validation.
