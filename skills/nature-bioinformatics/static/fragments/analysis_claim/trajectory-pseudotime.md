# Analysis claim: trajectory or pseudotime

## Safe claim structure

`Trajectory analysis ordered [cell population] along [inferred process],
consistent with [transition/state change], with [validation or limitation].`

## Required support

- Method and root/terminal state rationale.
- Cell population included and excluded.
- Genes or modules changing along pseudotime.
- Validation from time course, lineage tracing, perturbation, or known markers
  when making developmental or causal claims.

## Risk controls

- Pseudotime is not chronological time by default.
- Branches are hypotheses unless supported by orthogonal evidence.
- Do not imply lineage fate from cross-sectional data alone.
