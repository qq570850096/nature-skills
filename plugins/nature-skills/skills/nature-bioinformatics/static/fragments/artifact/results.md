# Artifact: Results prose

## Output job

Write Results text as an evidence ladder:

1. Define the biological question and dataset context.
2. State the observation with the exact population and comparison.
3. Give quantitative or figure support.
4. Interpret conservatively.
5. Name the boundary or validation status.

## Style

- Use past tense for performed analyses.
- Keep claims close to figures, statistics, and cohorts.
- Prefer "consistent with" or "suggesting" when evidence is computational.
- Do not move limitations to the Discussion if they are needed to understand the
  result.
